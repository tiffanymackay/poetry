About the Collection

"Caught in a Hazy Dream" explores life's fleeting moments through the lens of Tanka poetry, a traditional Japanese form known for its brevity and depth. The collection is divided into five themed sections:

  • Memory: Reflecting on past moments that shape our present.
  • Love: Capturing the essence of relationships and emotional connections.
  • Growth: Documenting personal development and self-discovery.
  • Transformation: Embracing change and new beginnings.
  • Dreamscapes: Blurring the lines between reality and imagination.

Each poem is paired with beautiful digital watercolor art, enhancing the visual and emotional impact of the words. Here's a glimpse of what you'll find inside:

endless blue above
mountains blur like watercolors—
on the picnic blanket
I drift off, caught between
reality and a hazy dream

This collection is perfect for poetry lovers who appreciate a modern take on a classic form, those who enjoy brief yet impactful verses, and anyone interested in exploring the spaces between waking and dreaming, past and present.
