About Tiffany Mackay

Tiffany is a Sacramento-based poet specializing in tanka, haiku, and other short-form poetry. Her work explores themes of memory, love, nature, and self-discovery, capturing the beauty of fleeting moments with gentle contemplation.

A keen observer of the natural world and human experience, she finds inspiration in seasonal changes, light and shadow interplay, and everyday revelations.

Her poetry invites readers to pause and rediscover the profound in the ordinary, bridging the gap between the external world and inner emotional landscapes.

Through her poetry, Tiffany seeks to evoke feelings of tranquility and reflection, creating moments of zen that resonate deeply with her audience. Her words are an invitation to pause, breathe, and find beauty in the fleeting moments of life.
