haiku & micro poetry

seaside air—
playground laughter
and coffee scent

wildflower countryside
waving back
at me

summer sun
bursting with gratitude—
every inch of me

quiet cove—
a sailboat
sails past its mooring

Seattle spring—
a continuous
drizzle

midday sun—
in the wildflowers
a crow

snow-covered mountains
tucked into a bed of clouds—
having overslept

swaying side to side
ocean currents shift our course—
the whims of the sea

Spring clouds parting
opening to a vivid sky—
a world in color

early summer—
seaweed swaying back and forth
below the swirling waves

calm waters—
a sailboat
through the trees

sleepy seaside—
a seagull
announces its presence

tanka & longer poetry

glittering stars
bring back to memory
our first kiss
while sitting on the shore
of the man-made lake

your hazel eyes
reflect the blue in your shirt
blinking—i return
from the first
date we had

the June sun
sets in the horizon
like a golden drop
of liquid honey
sweetening the world
